1 Important: About this summary

We are explore the difference in power between ACAT and our combination. We currently identified two cases where ACAT outperforms us:

  1. Signal is sparse and there is a super significant one (nothing we can do)
  2. There are some bad-behaved genewise null distributions, (that might be filtered by qc), if we include them in the combination, they will blur the combined distributions, which greatly affect the power.

This summary is about solving the 2. thing.
